LEGEND - every cell is a 2-char code: 1st char = material, 2nd char = orientation.

  other directional blocks (doors, trapdoors when open, torches, ladders, beds, chests...): ^ > v < = facing;
           closed trapdoors _ bottom / ~ top; u/d = facing up/down; h = hanging; doubled char = no orientation
  .. = air, ,, = untouched natural ground. Auto-computed props (stair shape, fence/pane/wall connections,
