# Text-only build quality

This document records the 1.1.0 revision. The subsequent object-validation and focused-review
changes, including current verification results, are documented in [focused-review.md](focused-review.md).

The first quality revision keeps `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, the compiled operation DSL,
standard as the default mode, and the existing standard/extra reasoning settings. It needs only
the existing Java runtime and Paper API. There is no image input, new model, Node process,
rendering service, or additional planning call in the generation path.

## What changed

### Architecture and style

The generation prompt now distinguishes massing, silhouette, proportions, hierarchy, opening rhythm,
material roles, circulation and appropriate detail. A short `plan.design` contract records those choices
in the existing compilation response. It expresses intent; reviewers must inspect its actual execution.

Universal quotas for four materials and three facade depth layers were removed. Timber framing,
shutters, planters, porches, chimneys and steep roofs are choices, rather than requirements on every
style or object. The `building` operation has independent `framing`, `windows`, `shutters`, `sills`,
`planters`, `porch` and `lighting` booleans, all defaulting to true for compatibility, plus
`window_spacing` from 3 to 16. Turning off automatic windows allows custom openings with existing
operations. Planters always retain their supporting sills. Disabling lighting delegates lighting to
the construction program. This is still a rectangular single-storey envelope; arbitrary room/roof
composition and a furniture placement solver are subsequent work.

### Spatial evidence for a text model

`SpatialReport` adds computed evidence ahead of existing exact coordinate layers:

- East-west and north-south height profiles.
- Walkable zones with bounds and connection to the surrounding site.
- Door connections between zones.
- Approach reachability for beds, storage and workstations; opaque obstruction above chests.

The report is deterministic, capped at 6,500 characters and skips its detailed grid above 1.2 million
cells. It never labels its output a beauty score. Walkability uses a whole-block approximation,
two-block headroom, one-block steps, ladders and doors treated as open. Zones can include outdoor
areas and roof platforms; they are not certified enclosed rooms. Rails, partial blocks, complex
stair geometry and interaction-specific reach can differ from this approximation. A negative
observation requires coordinate inspection before the reviewer calls it a defect.

### Review and acceptance

Reviewers assess separate criteria on an anchored 0–4 scale: fidelity, soundness, composition,
coherence, craft and usability, filtered for specialist roles. Each applicable criterion needs a
rating of at least 3 and specific observed evidence. A high overall self-score cannot compensate
for a failed criterion. Counts of materials, fixtures and decorative blocks remain descriptive
heuristics; facade concentration/flatness reports no longer prescribe decoration automatically.

Every assigned checklist item must be verified. Failed operations, stale merge conflicts and
unresolved critical/major reviewer findings block completion. Exact repair proofs can certify a
serious issue only when the issue names the same failed checklist item whose predicates pass.
Proofs are checked again after merging. The old completion shortcuts based on tiny patches or
repeatedly failing checklist items were removed. Review patches introducing new analyzer blocking
codes/severities are rejected. These checks do not establish that a same-code defect did not grow,
or that a subjective rating is correct; those remain limitations.

Review history contains at most two recent rounds per role and omits earlier self-scores. If a
later round makes no changes while acceptance still fails, verification stops rather than
repeating unchanged evidence. Existing round caps remain in force. Each call still combines
diagnosis with proposed repair operations: this revision does not introduce a separate critic API
call. Code makes the acceptance decision from the returned evidence and actual model checks.

### Drafts and rejected edits

Generated versions persist an `approved` boolean. Legacy and manually imported versions leave it
unknown; they are not retroactively certified. Failed new creations remain available as explicitly
labelled drafts, including in chat, `/aib list` and `/aib info`. They can be edited normally.

A failed edit/rebuild does not append a version. Its attempted model, plan, quality ratings and
diagnostics are saved under `plugins/AIBuild/drafts/`. The prior model and design metadata are
restored. Publication appends a version only after placement succeeds. Existing builds and world
contents are not regenerated by installing this revision. Drafts are not hidden from the world:
the existing live preview remains enabled and unapproved new creations are visibly marked drafts.

Metrics identify `pipelineRevision: text-quality-v1`, model, criterion assessments, blockers,
approval, calls, tokens and elapsed time. Raw reviews remain in the existing transcripts.

## Verification

The Maven suite has 27 passing tests, including new cases for dishonest `done=true`/99-score
responses, unrelated/failed proofs, missing or fractional rubric ratings, incomplete checklists,
failed operations, merge conflicts, structural regressions, blocked routes and individual furniture,
report bounds/determinism, restrained styles, recessed entrances and persisted draft state.

An envelope's entrance constraint follows an explicitly replaced door at the same x/y up to three
blocks behind the original facade after initial compilation. Later automatic passage repairs then
protect that intended entrance instead of recreating a second door at the original wall. Entrances
outside this limited relationship still need ordinary custom geometry rather than inferred relocation.

Existing operation, orientation, large-build, analysis, mode, checklist, placement, coordinate and
LLM transport checks also pass. On an isolated Paper 26.3 server, controlled response fixtures tested:

1. An accepted creation.
2. A rejected edit with `done=true`, score 99 and a major finding. Its previous version and plan
   survived; a separate rejected draft was saved. All 720 persisted model blocks matched the world
   after rollback and a flush.
3. A failed new creation persisted with `approved=false` and the explicit draft chat message.

These fixtures test control flow, not generated architectural quality. Real DeepSeek trials are
recorded separately below. Individual trials do not establish a reliable preference or latency
advantage. Better text evidence cannot guarantee visual beauty, originality, or eliminate
same-model judging bias; in-game inspection and blind owner comparisons remain necessary.

## Research basis

- [LMBuild](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04292v1): functional specifications and explicit part roles;
  extra revision rounds gave limited gains in that benchmark.
- [Holodeck §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09067v2): relational constraints and local layout
  solving outperform direct absolute-coordinate placement in its indoor-scene ablation. This
  motivates future furniture solving; no Minecraft advantage is claimed yet.
- [Self-preference bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06996): rubric-based judging can remain biased,
  which is why the model's grades are not treated as external validation.
- [MineCEraft](https://github.com/SewoongLee/mineCEraft): useful MIT-licensed construction checks,
  not an automatic measure of architectural creativity.

## Next experiments

First compare the original small-house prompt and a deliberately restrained modern-house prompt.
Then use a small set of bridges, multi-room houses, castles and non-building objects. Prefer named
components and anchors, independently configurable facade bays, constraint-based furnishing and
cheap competing massing concepts over adding more blind full-model review rounds. A solver or
larger library should be retained only if usability improves without flattening stylistic variety.

## Observations from October 31, 2026

The original small-house prompt completed in **225.548 seconds (3m46s)**, with three DeepSeek
standard calls, 51,290 input tokens, 34,955 output tokens (28,883 reasoning tokens) and 863 blocks.
It passed the new criterion gates and ended with zero critical or major analyzer findings.
After flushing the isolated world, all **863/863** saved model blocks matched actual world blocks.
The previous observation was 211.647 seconds, so this trial is slightly slower; it does not
demonstrate a speed improvement or a reliable aesthetic preference. The trials also overlap with
another generation and are not controlled latency measurements.

The modern 11x9 white-concrete/dark-oak trial used the new style controls and designed custom
asymmetric windows with no timber frames, shutters, planters or porch. It exposed a pre-existing
entrance constraint that recreated a door in front of the deliberately recessed entrance.
The first two critics also identified shell gaps and an inaccessible bedroom. This trial was
stopped after those diagnostics; it is **not** reported as an accepted generation or complete
timing benchmark. Its saved program and two model-authored repair patches were replayed through
the corrected local compiler/postprocessor without new AI calls. That replay has no duplicate
front door, has the required bed/crafting/chest approaches reachable in the approximation, and
reports zero critical or major analyzer issues. It has not received a fresh final AI acceptance
or a blind human preference evaluation. One extra barrel still has no reachable approach.

The real trials used the candidate build before final diagnostic-message changes, the recessed
entrance fix and the release version bump. Replays and regression tests use the final 1.1.0 code.
Raw measurements, model programs, analysis and human-viewable renders are under the ignored
`bench/text-quality-20261031/` directory. Those images are never sent to DeepSeek.

Local spatial-report observations on earlier saved models: 1,643 characters / 35 ms for the small
house, 3,376 characters / 133 ms for the castle. These are single process runs with approximate
offline block properties, not a throughput benchmark.
