# HANDBOOK: scale — the grid is metres
1 block = 1 m, and a player is 2 blocks tall, standing 1.6 m at eye level. Wrong scale ruins a build before any
detail is placed. Measure by these numbers:
- Doors 2 high (3-4 for halls, gates 4-7); window sills ~1 above the floor, windows 2-3 high.
- A storey is 4-5 blocks from floor to floor (3 clear inside + floor/beam layer); halls 6-9; a castle hall 10-14.
- Ceilings at least 3 clear; stairs need 3 clear above every step.
- A cottage is 7-11 wide; a townhouse 6-9 wide and 2-4 storeys; a manor 15-25; a keep 12-20 square, 25-40 high;
  a curtain wall 8-14 high, 3-5 thick with a walkway; towers stand 4-8 above the wall they flank.
- Trees: an oak 8-14, a big oak 15-25, a giant/hero tree 25-45 with a trunk 3-7 wide at the base.
- Statues: decide the height first, then proportions from it (a human is ~7.5 heads; head = height/7.5).
- Detail needs room: a feature under 3 blocks wide cannot carry an arch, a frame and a sill at once. When the
  request says "huge", detail grows with the size: a 60-block keep gets 2-block mouldings, not 1-block lines.
- Scale against the player, not against the plot: a door, a window and a stair step must still be player-sized
  on a giant build, and that contrast is what makes it read as huge.
