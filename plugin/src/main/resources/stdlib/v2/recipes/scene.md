# RECIPE: scene / village / landscape / island / city / several buildings
- Composition: one focal element (the biggest or tallest), 2-6 supporting elements, paths that connect them and
  lead the eye, open space between groups; vary sizes, heights, rotations and roof directions.
- Village: buildings (volume()) facing a square or a street, a well/fountain in the square, market stalls,
  fences and gardens, trees between houses, lamp posts along paths. Never identical copies: change floors,
  roofs, palette roles (alt roof blocks, wall mixes) and sizes.
- Terrain: terrain() relief, pond()/rivers, rocks, groundCover() across the whole scene; things stand on
  the terrain height (h(x, z) returned by terrain()).
- Scale: scenes are 60-160 across; every element still gets its own details.
