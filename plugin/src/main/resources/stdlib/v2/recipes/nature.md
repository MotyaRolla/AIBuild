# RECIPE: tree / giant tree / forest / garden / park / treehouse
- Custom trees only (tree() with species, h, crown): vary heights and species, group them in clumps of 3-5 with
  one dominant tree, never in a grid. A giant tree: h 25-40, roots spreading on the ground, thick branches, a
  hollow or doors in the trunk, platforms and rope bridges (fences/chains) for treehouses.
- Ground: groundCover() everywhere with forest:true under trees, terrain() for gentle relief, rocks in groups,
  ferns/grass/flowers in clusters, fallen logs (line() with axisOf), mushrooms, a stream or pond().
- Garden: paths of mixed blocks, hedges (bush), flower beds bordered by trapdoors/slabs, benches, lamp posts,
  a fountain or gazebo as the focal point.
