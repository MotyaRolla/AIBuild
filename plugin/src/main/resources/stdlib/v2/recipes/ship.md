# RECIPE: ship / boat / airship / vehicle / train / car
- Hull: loft() through 6-10 sections (narrow bow, full midship, rounded stern), shell 1-2, smooth:true; a keel,
  gunwale stripe (a darker/lighter row), stern castle and forecastle raised, a figurehead.
- Rigging: masts as line()/tube logs tapering, yards across, curved sails (white_wool shells of a bent surface),
  rigging lines with fences/chains, flags; crow's nest.
- Deck: planks with a trim border, hatches (trapdoors), barrels, cannons (polished blackstone + stairs), wheel.
- Airship: a balloon envelope (ellipsoid/loft shell, banded wool stripes), gondola hanging on chains,
  propellers (blades of slabs around a hub), steam pipes and copper details.
- Vehicles: correct proportions, wheels as cylinders with rims, windows recessed, lights, chrome trim.
- Setting: water around ships (shallow sea with gentle waves of slabs), a dock, or clouds for airships.
