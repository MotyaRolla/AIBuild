# RECIPE: bridge / aqueduct / viaduct / pier
- Structure that reads: arches (arch()) or piers with cutwaters on the upstream side, a deck with parapets and
  a cornice of upside-down stairs, lamp posts or statues at the ends, a slight hump for stone bridges.
- Wooden bridges: posts/piles as logs, cross braces, plank deck with trim, rope railings (fences/chains).
- Setting matters: banks with terrain(), water or a ravine under it, rocks, reeds, trees on the banks, the
  road continuing on both sides (path()).
