# RECIPE: statue / sculpture / monument / bust / figure
- Anatomy and proportions first: get head/torso/limb ratios right (a human is ~7.5 heads tall), give it a pose
  (weight on one leg, turned head, raised arm, flowing cloak) — never a stiff symmetric pillar.
- Build big enough for detail: 25-60 tall; a pedestal 1/6-1/4 of the height with mouldings (stairs/slabs) and
  steps; a plaza (groundCover or paving) around it.
- Form: blend() capsules, ellipsoids, tubes and lofts with smooth:true; no visible primitives; carve folds and
  hollows (sub / carve) for clothes, eyes, mouths.
- Shading: shade() every mass (stone ramp for marble/bronze/stone statues; a coloured ramp for painted ones).
  Brighter faces up and toward the viewer, darker under arms, chin, cloak folds.
- Signature details: a face (eyes, brows, nose), held objects (sword, book, staff), armour plates, hair locks.
