# RECIPE: pagoda / temple / Japanese or Chinese building / torii / garden
- Pagoda: 3-7 stacked tiers, each smaller than the one below, each with a wide flared roof (overhang 2,
  upturned corners: one extra stair raised at the four corners), dark tile roofs, red/dark-oak posts, white walls.
- Temple: raised stone base with steps on all sides, a big hip roof with generous overhang and curved eaves,
  posts on the outer gallery, lanterns.
- Garden: pond() with stepping stones, a red arched bridge (tube or arch), stone lanterns (wall + lantern + slab),
  raked gravel, cherry and pine trees (tree species cherry / pine), moss, rocks in groups of 3.
- Inside: tatami-like floors (birch or bamboo planks with a carpet border), low tables (slabs) with cushions
  (carpets), paper screens (white wool/birch trapdoors), lanterns; a temple hall gets an altar (furnish 'chapel').
- Palette: palette('japanese'); accents red_terracotta / red concrete for torii and bridges.
