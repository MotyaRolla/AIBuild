# RECIPE: animal / creature / dragon / monster
- Recognizability: list the 3-5 features that make the species unmistakable (silhouette, ears, snout, tail,
  wings, horns, markings) and exaggerate them slightly. Count only features that show on the subject as asked:
  one covered by what it wears, holds or does stays covered, and the covering thing sits on it with no gap.
- Pose and life: a dynamic pose (turned head, lifted paw, curled tail, open wings, crouch) and an action or
  setting (perched on a rock, guarding a hoard, curled on a cushion). Never stand it stiffly in empty space.
- Size from the smallest feature that must read: eyes 1-2 blocks each with at least 1 block between them and a
  nose or muzzle below, so a face is 7+ wide and the head 7+ wide. The rest follows the species' real
  proportions to that head in this pose (how much wider and longer the body is, how a sitting or crouching body
  widens to its base, how long the legs and tail are), never a body cut to the head's width. A small animal (cat, fox, owl) is a statue
  15-25 tall; "small" in the request means the low end of that, never below it. Big creatures (dragon, whale)
  40-80 long, with finer anatomy (toes, claws, teeth, scales via shade/mix, eye whites).
- Form: tube() spines/necks/tails with tapering radius, blend() torso + limbs + head, loft() bodies; wings as
  thin membranes between finger bones (tubes) with shell/carve; smooth:true on all organic masses.
- Colour: shade() the coat on a ramp of its colour; belly/undersides a lighter or contrasting ramp, markings
  painted with paint(); eyes 1-2 blocks with a highlight block, painted from the side they face
  (paint(shape, m, {from: 'south'})) on a flat face plane so they stay one flush layer (handbook organic).
- Setting: a base it sits or stands on (rock, cushion, nest, hoard, a patch of ground) and a little around it,
  lower than the creature and never hiding it from the front; anything taller (a tree) stands behind it and
  well away. The creature dominates every view.
