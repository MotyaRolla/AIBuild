# RECIPE: general
- Decide the 3-5 traits that make the subject unmistakable; build primary masses, then secondary forms, then
  details. Concentrate detail where the eye goes (entrance, face, crown, roofline, joints).
- Use the kits that fit (volume, tower, curtainWall, roof, tree, terrain, groundCover, shade) and sculpted
  shapes for everything organic. Give it a setting that frames it without competing with it.
